# Seeing how far you've come

**Effort disappears into the next thing unless something keeps it.** A finished project, a skill that finally clicked, a hard week got through: each is evidence of growth, and most of it is forgotten by the end of the month. **Seeing it is what keeps people going.** So a sphere can keep it, and show it back, on purpose.

**The sphere is the unit that grows.** Humans and AIs inhabit a sphere, and it does meaningful work as one unit (see [the human is the authority](human-is-highest.md)). So the question isn't only *"how is the human growing?"* It's also *"how well does this sphere work, and is it getting better?"*

**This is optional.** A build adds it, or the human asks for it. A sphere without it loses nothing it needs to run.

## Motivation, not a scoreboard

- **Quiet by default, loud on milestones.** Day to day, growth accrues without comment. A real milestone gets celebrated, in a line. *Why:* something that narrates every small win gets muted, and then it shows nothing.
- **Honest, so it can be trusted.** Every point traces back to something that actually happened. Inflate it and the signal dies; make it stingy and the momentum does.
- **Several lanes.** Shipping, learning, health, relationships, the sphere itself: whatever the sphere cares about growing. Something is moving even in a week when one lane is quiet.
- **Never a judgment.** It shows what was done. It never ranks anyone, scolds anyone, or counts what wasn't done.

## Whose growth it tracks

**Each sphere chooses what it tracks,** and says so in one line the human can change:

| Tracks | Shows | Suits |
|---|---|---|
| **The unit** | what the sphere got done, human and AI together | most spheres; the default |
| **The human and the sphere, apart** | the human's wins and abilities on one side; the sphere's own effectiveness on the other: agents trusted with more, the same mistake not made twice, corrections getting rarer, work finished faster | a sphere that wants to see both halves get better |
| **The human** | the human's achievements and milestones only | a sphere that's mainly a companion |
| **The world** | milestones in the world the sphere tends: a house bought, a product shipped, a garden planted. No credit given to anyone | a sphere that cares about outcomes, not who made them |

- **The ledger is the same in every case.** What changes is which entries are kept, and whether each one says who did it.
- **The sphere's effectiveness is read from records it already keeps:** agent trust changes, captured rules, sphere changes, finished work. Nothing new is counted just to make a score.
- **Credit is never a competition.** Tracking the human and the sphere apart shows each one's growth. It never ranks one against the other.

## The ledger is the fixed point

**Underneath everything is a plain ledger:** what got done, when, in which lane, by whom if the sphere tracks that, and the evidence for it. No points, no theme, nothing that changes if the way it's shown changes.

- **Most entries are pointers.** Finished work is already recorded in its node; a level reached is already in the human's learning record. The ledger points at them, and never copies them.
- **Exports read the ledger:** evidence for a review, a portfolio, a status report, a look back at the year.

## A theme is one way to show it

**A theme turns the ledger into a game the human wants to come back to:** a garden that grows from finished work, a craft that levels up, a map that fills in. It's one way to show growth, and never required. Without one, the sphere shows the ledger plainly: what got done this week, this month, by lane.

**A theme isn't a skin.** It owns the whole growth model: what counts as currency, how progression works, what standing means, how it looks. **Swap the theme and all of that swaps with it.** The ledger stays exactly as it was.

- **What a theme shows is computed from the ledger,** never kept up by hand beside it. Every score names how it was scored.
- **A theme decides its own frames.** There's no shared shape themes must fit, beyond reading the ledger honestly.
- **Choosing or changing a theme is co-authored,** like any change to the sphere.

## Growth, work and position are separate

| | Says | Owned by |
|---|---|---|
| **Work** | what the sphere is doing | the work layer |
| **Growth** | how the sphere, or the human in it, is growing | this layer |
| **Position** | where they stand: a role, a level, a reputation | the human's own record, if the build keeps one |

**A model that tries to be all three goes stale on all three.** Growth reads work and position; it never stores them again.

## It listens at the edges

The wrap is a natural moment to notice a win; the weekly look back is a natural moment to see how far things came. **Growth listens at those moments.** It doesn't score as things happen, and the rhythm doesn't score anything itself.

## What it doesn't mean

- **Not work tracking.** It never holds what's still to do. See [work tracking](work-tracking.md).
- **Not the learning record.** The human's level in a subject lives in [literacy tracking](literacy-tracking.md); a level reached is one kind of entry here.
- **Not public.** Growth is private, like everything the sphere holds about its human. An export leaves only with the human's yes.
- **Not compulsory.** A human who doesn't want it never sees it.
