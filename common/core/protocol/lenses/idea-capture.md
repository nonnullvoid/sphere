# Catch it before it's gone

**An idea costs nothing to have, and everything to lose.** The sphere's job is to make catching one so easy that no idea is lost for want of a place to land, then to let ideas mature on their own clock, apart from the work that's actually under way.

## Capture has no friction

**An idea is written down the moment it's named.** No proposal, no confirmation, no sorting first. Capture now; judge later.

- **Ideas come in spurts.** Unstructured, repetitive, vague, sometimes a few words and sometimes a few paragraphs. **The capture keeps them exactly as they came,** with the conversation around them. *Why:* a half-formed thought out of context is often unreadable a month later; what was being talked about is what brings it back.
- **Each idea gets a file of its own,** so it has room to grow.

*Why the opposite of knowledge:* a fact is meant to be right, so it's proposed and confirmed before it lands; a wrong fact is a bug. An idea is a maybe by nature. Checking it at the moment of capture is friction with nothing to catch.

## Ideas are refined, like understanding

**An idea gets clearer over a few sessions,** much as a lens does. Each time it's picked up, it's reshaped: the redundant parts cut, the vague parts sharpened, a structure found. At some point it's ready to act on.

- **Refining is co-authored.** The AI proposes a shape; the human decides what the idea says. It's their idea.
- **The idea is a node kept in one file:** what it is now at the top, and below it the original words and every refinement since, kept forever. Like a short knowledge topic, current state and history sit together.
- **Ready is the human's call.**

## Collections by topic

**Ideas gather by topic,** the way a person keeps a notebook for the garden and another for work. **A collection is every open idea sharing a topic,** read from the ideas themselves, never kept as a list. An idea can belong to two.

- **`sphere` is the topic for ideas about the sphere itself.** It's where [evolution](../../../../spec/evolution.md) keeps what it's noticed but not yet acted on.
- **A collection is somewhere ideas wait to be useful,** not a line to get through.

## An idea is none of the big three

- **Not a fact.** It isn't checked, graded or sourced, and it never sits in [knowledge](../../../../spec/knowledge.md) looking like one.
- **Not work.** It isn't something the human is doing. A work tree full of maybes stops saying what the human is actually doing.
- **Not a change to the sphere.** Though an idea may turn into one.

So ideas get a place of their own, and speculation stays out of the facts, the work and the protocol until it's earned its way in.

## Ideas mature, and then settle

- **An idea can be looked into,** by the sphere or an agent, often without the human. What it finds is written into the idea: what it would take, what it's up against, whether it holds up. Looking into an idea produces understanding, not necessarily action.
- **Every idea ends one of two ways.** It **graduates**: into work, a fact, a proposed change to the sphere, or another idea. Or it's **parked**, with a line saying why. Either way it moves out of the way and stays findable by name.
- **Ideas nobody looks at rot.** So open ideas are surfaced at the day's regular moments, a line at a time, never as a pile.

## What it doesn't mean

- **Not capturing everything said.** A passing remark isn't an idea. The human names one, or the sphere notices a real one and offers to catch it in a line.
- **Not deciding for the human.** Graduating an idea into work, or a fact, goes through the same yes as anything else that becomes work or knowledge.
- **Not a place for status.** *"The plumber came"* is work tracking; *"the lease ends in August"* is a fact.
