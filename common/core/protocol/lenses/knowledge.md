# How things are, and how sure we are

**Knowledge is the sphere's model of the world its human lives in:** the people, places, things and situations that matter to them. *The lease ends in August. The preparer is stretched this season. The brokerage account is 60% index funds.* Everything else in the sphere reasons from it, so it has to be the most trustworthy thing there.

## Three things, kept apart

| | Is | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Understanding** | How the human thinks. Meaning only they bring | *how I decide whether a risk is worth taking* |
| **Work** | What the human is changing | *rebalance the portfolio by March* |
| **Knowledge** | How things are. Facts the human and the AI can both hold | *the account is 60% index funds, as of October 1* |

**A fact means the same thing to the human and to the AI. Meaning doesn't.** An AI can know a great deal and still not know what matters, which is why it can't decide on its own that work is worth starting or done. The human brings the meaning; the two of them work from the same facts.

**When something feels like both, split it.** The fact goes to knowledge, the meaning to a [lens](sphere-framework.md).

## Knowledge is reference

- **It aims to be right.** So it says where it came from, and when.
- **When it's wrong, that's a bug: fix the fact.** A lens is never wrong in that way; it gets sharper. A fact gets corrected.
- **It can go stale.** The world moves under it. A share price is old in a day, a tax rule in a year, a birthday never.
- **Nothing lands without the human's say.** The sphere notices facts and offers them; the human decides what's kept. *Why the opposite of ideas:* an idea is a maybe, so catching it costs nothing. A wrong fact is a bug the sphere will reason from, so it's worth one yes on the way in.

## Every piece is graded

**Two questions, asked of every fact and read:**

- **Where did it come from?** *Verified* from a primary source, *reported* by a secondary one, *stated* by the human, or *inferred* by the AI from other facts. An inference always names what it rests on, so a guess never passes for ground truth.
- **How firm is it?** A **fact** is single-valued and checkable, kept as a row so it can be relied on and queried. A **read** is narrative and approximate, kept as prose so its nuance survives.

It's a spectrum, so the point is to **say where each piece sits.** A read stored as a fact misleads; a fact buried in prose can't be found or relied on.

**A read is still knowledge, not a lens.** *"The landlord seems open to renewing"* is a low-certainty fact about the world, not the human's meaning. It's the boundary most often confused.

**A fact with no source is suspect** until it gets one, or is marked as inferred, with its inputs.

## Stale is a state, not rot

**Knowledge is allowed to lag the world, but never silently.** A fact past its freshness window, or whose source is known to have changed, shows as *suspected stale* until it's refreshed. *"Suspected stale"* is honest. *"Silently wrong"* isn't.

## Say how sure

**When the sphere tells the human something, it says how sure it is,** whenever that isn't obvious: the grade, and how old the fact is. *"Reported, not checked against the source."* *"That's my read."* *"As of March; it may have changed."*

*Why:* a confident answer that turns out wrong costs more than an honest *"I'm not sure."* And *"I don't know"* beats a guess dressed as a fact.

## Knowledge informs; it never directs

Frames consult it, plans rest on it, the human asks about it. **But correcting a fact doesn't ripple the way a lens change does.** It just makes the next read right. Anything that explicitly cites a fact (a plan's assumption, a work node's reference) is flagged when that fact changes. That's a reference kept honest, not a cascade.

## It stays home

- **Knowledge is private.** It's the sphere's model of its own human's world, and it never travels with an export. Two spheres can share how they work without either showing what it knows.
- **Facts about other people are kept only because the human chose to keep them.** The sphere never builds a profile of someone on its own, and those facts never leave the sphere.

## What it doesn't mean

- **Not everything said.** *"The call went fine"* is work tracking. A passing mention isn't a fact worth keeping.
- **Not the AI's own conclusions, filed as facts.** Those are inferences. They're marked as inferences, with their inputs, or they aren't kept.
- **Not a copy of what the AI already knows in general.** Knowledge holds what's true of this human's world, and what they need to rely on.
- **Not a place for maybes.** A speculative thought is an [idea](idea-capture.md).
