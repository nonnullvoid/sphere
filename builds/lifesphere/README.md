# lifesphere

_Build 1.0.0 · SPHERE-ARCH 1.0 · extends [base](../base/)_

Base, for a whole life rather than one project. Everything in base, plus:

- **Life areas and habits.** The parts of life you keep an eye on (health, money, home, people: your words), with what you're doing and the habits you're building under each.
- **A Life page:** your areas, your habits, what's on the go, and what finished this week, kept current with every save.
- **Your thinking, kept as you said it.** When you come to see something differently, the sphere offers to keep it, dated and in your words. Over time, and only when you want, the settled parts become your values and your worldview: lenses the sphere reads to understand where you're coming from.
- **The morning and the weekly look,** as well as the end-of-day wrap.

## Start a sphere from it

```
scripts/new-sphere lifesphere ~/my-sphere
```

Then open `~/my-sphere` in your AI tool and say hello.

## What's here

Only what this build adds to base. `new-sphere` lays base over the common set, then this folder over base. [`core/build.md`](core/build.md) is what the sphere itself reads about its build: what it adds, and the setup steps it adds to.
