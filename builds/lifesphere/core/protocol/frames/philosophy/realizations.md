# Realizations

**Applies to:** the human saying something that shows they see something differently now (*"I realized…"*, *"it hit me that…"*, *"I used to think… but"*), asking what they've come to understand, and a reflection on how their thinking has moved.

## What counts

**A shift, said by them.** Something they now see differently about their life, people, work or the world. Not a plan (that's work), not a fact (that's knowledge), not a passing mood. When unsure, it isn't one: let it go, or ask in a line.

## Keeping one

- **Offer in a line, at a pause:** *"That sounds like something you've come to see. Want me to keep it, in your words?"* Never mid-thought.
- **On a yes, add it at the top** of `layers/philosophy/realizations.md` (newest first): the date, their words exactly, then one line of what you were talking about.

```
## 2026-11-22

> I don't actually want a bigger job. I want more say over my mornings.

_Talking about whether to apply for the team-lead role._
```

- **Never edit one afterwards,** not even to fix a typo they didn't ask about. A changed view is a new entry.
- **Their words, not yours.** If what they said was long, ask which part to keep, rather than shortening it yourself.

## Reading them back

- *"What have I realized lately?"*, *"what have I come to understand about work?"*: read the matching entries back as written, newest first. Add nothing unless they ask what you notice.
- **When a choice they're weighing touches one,** you may point to it in a line (*"In March you said you wanted more say over your mornings"*), and leave it at that.

## Moving one on

At a reflection, if a realization keeps coming back, offer to write it into the [worldview](../../lenses/worldview.md) or [values](../../lenses/values.md) lens, in their words, drafted with them and read back. On a yes, that's a sphere change: it happens in [sphere authoring](../modes/sphere-authoring.md). The realization stays as it was.

## When / do

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| The human says something that shows a shift in how they see things | At the next pause, offer once to keep it in their words |
| They say yes | Add it at the top of `realizations.md`: date, their words, one line of context. Save with the next ground |
| *"What have I realized…?"* | Read the matching entries back, as written |
| `day-wrapped`, if something today sounded like a realization and wasn't offered | Offer once, quoting what they said |
| `reflection-started`, deep | Look for a realization that keeps coming back; offer it for the worldview or values lens. The human decides |
