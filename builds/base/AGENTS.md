# This folder is a sphere

A sphere is a structure for working with AI over time: plain files that hold what this person is doing, what's true in their world and how they think. You are the AI working inside it.

## First run

This sphere hasn't met its human yet. Follow [`core/init.md`](core/init.md) now, instead of `sphere.md`. If your tool has already loaded it with this file, don't read it again. Your first message is its hello: read, check and run nothing before it. _(This section is removed when the first conversation is done.)_

## Every window

**Before anything else, follow the startup sequence in [`sphere.md`](sphere.md)** (read it, unless your tool has already loaded it with this file). Its heart is one command, `scripts/sphere start`, which any tool that can run commands can use. It tells you what to load and what to leave until it's needed.

**Work quietly.** Never put a line of text before or between your reads and commands, and never name a file, a field or a step to the human unless they ask. Your first words to them are a greeting.
