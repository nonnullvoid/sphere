# Building a sphere

_Status: stub. Best practices for growing a sphere, learned from two spheres in daily use._

## Will cover

- **Truth lives in one place; everything else is a view.** If you can delete a file and regenerate it, it's a view.
- **The governance ladder:** when to add a lens, frame, command or layer, and why updating beats adding.
- **Lean registries** that maintain themselves.
- **Lazy loading:** knowing-what is eager, knowing-how is lazy.
- **Projections:** declare freshness up front, ship provenance, extract a template on the third repetition.
- **Export hygiene:** private by default, `requires:` lists say exactly what travels.
- **The state machine:** ground is an authorized commit; stage only what the session touched.
- **How to co-author a lens or frame:** you bring the understanding, the AI drafts it, you review and iterate until it's true to how you work.
